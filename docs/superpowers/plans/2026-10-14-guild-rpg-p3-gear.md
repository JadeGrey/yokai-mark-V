# Guild RPG Phase 3 (Gear) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** executed natively and inline (the owner delegated plans and execution). Each task is test-first: tests, watch them fail, implement, run, lint, commit.

**Goal:** Gear makes the game a loot RPG: items with rolled stats and durability, five equipment slots, eight tags with set bonuses and skills, mission drops, the daily guild shop, repairs, and `/gear` commands. Characters get stronger from gear, so enemy power is re-tuned.

**Architecture:** Item content is data (generated into `content/rpg/items.toml` by a script that a test keeps in sync). Pure modules compute rolled stats, loadouts (final stats, set bonuses, granted skills), durability and prices, drops and shop stock. The store gains item, equipment and shop tables and does equip, wear, drop, buy, sell and repair atomically with the Renown ledger. Fights read each player's loadout and, on settlement, apply wear and add drops in the same transaction.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-14-guild-rpg-design.md` (sections 5, 6, 7, 10, 12, 14, 15 and phase 3 of 16, plus the notes in section 18).

## Decisions made while planning (recorded in the spec in the last task)

- **Rarities:** common, uncommon, rare, epic. Shop stock is common and uncommon only; rarer gear drops.
- **Tiers:** five gear tiers with level requirements 1, 12, 24, 38 and 52, one per couple of ranks.
- **800 item definitions** (8 tags x 5 slots x 5 tiers x 4 rarities) are generated by `scripts/gen_rpg_items.py`; `tests/test_rpg_gear_content.py` fails if `items.toml` and the generator disagree. Edit the generator, not the file.
- **Stats:** an item's budget is `power_per_level x level requirement x slot weight x rarity multiplier`, spread over the tag's three "leans" stats, minus a small tag-specific drawback stat (the nerf). Rolled values vary by +/- `roll_percent` around the base and are stored on the instance.
- **Set bonuses:** a tag's 2-piece and 4-piece bonuses are percentages of the final stat (Crit bonuses are flat points). Only items above 0 durability count. The 4-piece also grants the tag's skill. Epic weapons carry one extra skill.
- **Durability** is out of 100 (epic more). After every fight each equipped item loses `win_wear` or `loss_wear` points. Warnings are shown when an item crosses 50%, 25% or 0%, in the result and before starting a mission. A full repair costs `repair_percent` of the item's price, pro rata.
- **Inventory** holds at most `inventory_cap` items; further drops are sold automatically at `sell_percent`. `/gear sell` sells unequipped items for the same share.
- **Gear changes are blocked** while the member is in a fight.
- **Starting a mission with low or broken gear** shows a warning with Start/Cancel buttons before any attempt is spent.
- **Admin tools** (`/admin rpg ...`: grant an item, set level, set rank, reset a character) arrive in this phase because they are needed to test gear; they are audited like the other admin actions.
- **Renown reasons:** `rpg_reward` (existing), `rpg_repair`, `rpg_shop`, `rpg_sale`.

## Review Focus

1. **Duplicate items:** two simultaneous equips, buys or sells of one item must never duplicate or lose it (store tests).
2. **Atomic purchases and repairs:** a failed debit (not enough Renown) changes nothing; a purchase over the daily cap is refused (store tests).
3. **Wear and drops land once:** settling a fight twice applies wear and drops once (store tests).
4. **Broken gear counts for nothing:** an item at 0% contributes no stats, no tag count and no skill, and the loadout reflects that immediately (gear tests).
5. **Level requirements:** gear above the member's level cannot be equipped, even by direct id (store and command tests).
6. **Content drift:** retired items keep working, unknown content ids in the database are tolerated by `/gear inventory` and `/card`, and `items.toml` always matches the generator (content tests).
7. **Gear while fighting:** equip, unequip, repair and sell are refused during a fight (command tests).
8. **Inventory overflow:** a drop into a full inventory is auto-sold, never lost silently and never exceeds the cap (store tests).

## Tasks

1. Content: item, loot, shop and gear models and loaders; tag bonuses and player skills; the generator and the generated items file.
2. Pure gear logic: rolling, loadout and final stats, durability, prices, drops, shop stock.
3. Store (schema v11): items, equipment, shop, wear, drops, buying, selling, repairing; the debit helper.
4. Fight integration: loadouts and skills in fights, wear and drops at settlement, result and pre-mission warnings, gear on the card.
5. `/gear` commands, the inventory view, `/admin rpg` tools.
6. Simulator with gear, re-tuning of enemy power, prices and the economy report; pacing tests.
7. Docs, QA rows, spec notes, full verification.
